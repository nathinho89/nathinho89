## How I Work

**Scalable component systems**  
I build components with composition in mind — not just for the current requirement, but for the next three. That means thinking carefully about props APIs, slot composition, state ownership, and where abstraction genuinely pays off versus where it adds noise.

**Developer experience as a first-class concern**  
DX is product work. Slow feedback loops, confusing APIs, and inconsistent conventions drain velocity. I invest in tooling, documentation, and internal APIs that make the right thing easy to do.

**Fast feedback, consistently**  
I'm a strong advocate for tight feedback loops: component testing close to the source, visual regression awareness, PR conventions that surface issues early. The goal is a culture where quality is caught continuously, not at the end.

**Moving faster without sacrificing quality**  
The fastest path to quality isn't slowing down — it's better abstractions, shared patterns, and reducing the surface area for mistakes. I'm always looking for ways to trim friction from the process: better scaffolding, smarter defaults, automation where it earns its place.

**Mentoring and enabling teams**  
I've led and grown frontend teams in both agency and product contexts. I put a lot of effort into building the conditions for engineers to do their best work — through pairing, documentation, structured feedback, and creating space for engineers to own their craft.
